## Adapted to this original portfolio
- One persistent scene and camera instead of separate unrelated WebGL sections.
- Small legible engineered labels against editorial display type; local Italiana, DM Sans and Space Grotesk.
- Letter-by-letter hover transitions on compact controls, with restrained magnetic displacement.
- Distinct narrative cadence: stillness, pressure, fast release, suspended hold, weighted fall, recovery.
- Green drafting environment as a motivated material transition, not another generic section background.
- Project portal maintains the visitor's exact scroll position and offers an explicit return.
- Point-cloud text uses actual sampled typography, not an unrelated particle backdrop.

No third-party site code, proprietary artwork, branding or models are copied.
