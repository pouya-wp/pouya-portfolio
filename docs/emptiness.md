# THE EMPTINESS BUILD — design bible

One thesis, ten laws, four acts, under twenty words spoken.

## The thesis

> What is, has no essence of its own.
> The empty is not the absence — the empty is the part that works.
> And the price of the road is the removal of whoever came to gain something.

Attar: thousands set out, thirty arrive. The road adds nothing. It subtracts
everyone who was not going to arrive.

## The governing law (Wittgenstein §7)

The most important thing on this site is never stated.

- Under 20 words of philosophy in the entire site.
- Zero occurrences of: emptiness, void, śūnyatā, fanā, consciousness, journey,
  passion, mastery. Naming the concept means the execution failed.
- No age, no biography, no "my journey", and no date of birth. The colophon lists
  what the work cost, never what the maker is.
- If a visitor never learns there was a philosophy but felt it — we won.
  If they learned it but felt nothing — we lost.

## The ten laws

Laws are invisible. Acts are visible. A law is never a section; it is a rule the
whole system obeys. Ten sections would be noise. Ten laws are a world.

## Fletcher

There are no two words more harmful than praise. So the site never praises.
No success state, no tick, no confetti, no "nice". Ever.

The only words the system ever says to the visitor are two:

    YOU'RE RUSHING
    YOU'RE DRAGGING

And when the tempo is right, it says nothing at all. The silence is the reward.

Never quote the film. Never explain. The visitor works it out or does not.

## The thirty

Do not start at thirty. Arrive at thirty.

    fracture      →  ~340 fragments
    each valley   →  attrition, driven by the visitor's tempo
    final valley  →  exactly 30

The fragments are versions of the maker. Each carries a bare year and nothing
else. Most do not arrive. The thirty that do converge — and the shape they make
is the visitor's own path through the site. The seeker was the sought.

## Non-negotiables (usability is 30% of the score)

- Tempo never blocks and never loses the visitor's place. It only costs fragments.
- Reduced motion is a real path, not a broken one.
- Mobile: stillness reads as the finger leaving the glass. Attrition is gentler.
- 60fps on mid hardware or the law gets cut, whatever it costs conceptually.

## The footer

No name. No surname. One line:

    NOT THE MAKER. THE MADE.

No name, no initials, no date. The one line about the maker is the one that says
the maker was not what was on offer.

## Killed (this belongs in the colophon, not in a graveyard file)

- A CRT monitor that the whole interface lived inside — three days.
- A night room, moonlight, a hand lifting a cup — cut, the register was wrong.
- The history of the web collapsing decade by decade — cut, it was a second film.
